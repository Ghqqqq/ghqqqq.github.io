I am a PhD student at [ShanghaiTech University](https://www.shanghaitech.edu.cn/), advised by [Xin Liu](https://liuxincell.github.io/). My research centers on reinforcement learning and bandits, spanning theoretical foundations and applications to large language models and resource allocation.

Since mid-2025, I have been a research intern at Tencent CDG through the Tencent Rhino-Bird Elite Talent Program, advised by [Junwei Pan](https://junwei-pan.github.io/), working on reinforcement learning for recommendation and online bidding.

More recently, my attention has turned to reinforcement learning for agentic LLMs and multimodal large models, together with related questions on safe and efficient learning in these settings.
